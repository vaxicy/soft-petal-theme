Soft Petal — a calm and elegant browsing experience.

Open Chrome in a quiet room filled with soft flower petals and warm natural light. Soft Petal wraps your browser in a gentle pastel palette — cream toolbar, blush frame, light petal active tab, and soft pink accents. A minimal monochrome theme built for calm, clean, and premium everyday browsing.

Features:
- Soft petal palette: warm cream toolbar, blush frame, light petal active tab, gentle pink accents.
- Pure color theme — no wallpaper, no images, no gradients, just clean elegance.
- Comfortable contrast and good readability for daily use.
- Carefully tuned tints that feel soft, peaceful, and premium.

Let your browser breathe with the quiet comfort of soft petals.
